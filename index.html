3D Anatomy Viewers

Interactive anatomical models that run in the browser.

  • Female Reproductive System
    Rotate, zoom, and click individual structures to explore female sexual anatomy.
  • Male Reproductive System
    Rotate, zoom, and click individual structures to see their names and descriptions.
  • Neocortex
    The four lobes of the cerebral cortex, plus the insula, cerebellum and brainstem — click a lobe or pick one from the legend.
  • Limbic System
    Twenty-three structures inside a transparent cortex — hippocampus, amygdala, fornix, thalamus, basal ganglia and more. Select one and the rest fade back.
